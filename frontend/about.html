About TechNova

We build the web, thoughtfully.

A small studio that treats every website like production software — planned, tested, and built to last, not just to launch.

Our story

Started with one simple frustration

TechNova began as a reaction to slow, bloated, template-driven websites that looked the same as everyone else's and broke the moment real traffic hit them.

So we set out to do the opposite: hand-built front ends, backends that are actually tested, and a process where you always know what's happening with your project — no jargon, no black boxes.

Every site we ship is version-controlled, documented, and handed over in a state someone else could maintain — including you.

Our process

From first call to launch

The same five steps, every time — it's how we keep quality consistent across every project.

01

Discover

We start with a call to understand your goals, audience and constraints — no proposal goes out before we understand the problem.

02

Design

Wireframes and visual direction, reviewed with you before a single line of production code is written.

03

Build

Front end and backend built in parallel, with regular check-ins so there are no surprises at the end.

04

Test & Launch

Cross-browser and mobile testing, performance checks, then a controlled launch — not a last-minute scramble.

05

Support

We stay reachable after launch for fixes, updates and the occasional "can we add one more thing."
